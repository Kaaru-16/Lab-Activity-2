Gameplay

Turn-based strategy combat built around elemental Weakness Breaks and the seven Paths. Build a team, chain your turns, and topple bosses with precise timing.

Push your team in the Forgotten Hall, roll the dice in the Simulated Universe's roguelike runs, or just explore each new world at your own pace.
